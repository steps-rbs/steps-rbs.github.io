Overview

From a human goal to a frozen capability test.

Every robot shot is a path-traced replay of a recorded trial; every number on screen is taken from the paper.

STEPS overview film · 1080pReplays re-execute saved programs; simulator physics is unchanged, only the camera is chosen for presentation.

Code-as-Policy agents improve through practice. But a high score on a practiced task cannot tell us whether the agent gained a reusable capability or adapted to the instance it practiced.

STEPS makes the human goal the unit of evaluation. It generates physically valid, oracle-solvable task scenes from the goal, compiles the full goal into an L5 task, and derives held-out L1–L4 tasks from the same objects and relations. The agent develops its skill library on the goal; then the library is frozen and tested on the practiced task, on regenerated instances, and on the held-out tasks.

Framework

One goal, a ladder of held-out tests.

STEPS compiles the full goal from verified relation templates and derives held-out probes from the same objects and relations. The hierarchy is a measurement axis, not a curriculum.

01 / GROUND

Generate the task scene

A language model plans supports and the goal objects bound to them; floor assets follow zones and wall runs, and graph-edit correction fixes only the objects a VLM flags. Layered checks and a privileged oracle accept the scene.

02 / DERIVE

Compile L5, derive L1–L4

Goal relations from verified templates define the L5 success condition. L1–L4 tasks start from the L5 poses and are labeled goal subtask, component probe, or constraint probe.

03 / FREEZE

Develop, then freeze

The agent develops its skill library on L5 without restriction. The frozen agent is then evaluated on L5, regenerated instances, and the held-out L1–L4 tasks.

L1
Action

Lift or push one goal object.

L2
Relation

Satisfy one object's relation in a reduced scene.

L3
Composition

Satisfy several relations in a reduced scene.

L4
Preservation

Combine groups in the full scene; keep others in place.

L5
Full goal

Complete the human-specified goal.

Every derived task starts from its L5 object poses, so a failure cannot be blamed on a harder layout.

Goal subtasks

Relations that every successful L5 solution also satisfies.

Component probes

Capabilities that L5 solutions exercise but L5 success does not reveal.

Constraint probes

Extra preservation or geometry requirements the goal does not impose.

Human-directed task scenes

A goal becomes a scene the robot can act in.

Compared with Holodeck, SceneWeaver and SAGE on the same clean-up instructions ( scenes per method and room), STEPS is the only method without colliding objects in all three room types.

Layered validation

01

Asset settling. Each movable asset is dropped from 2 mm and must settle within 15° and 5 cm.

02

Scene at rest. After 2 s no object moves more than 5 cm or tilts more than 10°, and nothing interpenetrates by more than 5 mm.

03

Oracle solvability. A privileged oracle must solve the task in at least two of three seeds from non-trivial initial states.

04

Regeneration. The accepted scene is frozen and regenerated under controlled pose perturbations.

Interpretation

Measure the gap before claiming improvement.

Diagnosis, not a solution

STEPS diagnoses self-improvement but does not solve it: no tested evolution route yet reliably improves goal-level performance.

Human-assisted development

The strongest source library was developed with human assistance; it is evidence of execution reuse, not of autonomous skill discovery.

Protocols are not interchangeable

Five-room, RoboSuite and LIBERO-PRO trials use different models, interfaces, budgets and predicates, so their denominators are reported within each protocol.
